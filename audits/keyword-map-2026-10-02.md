## What the map actually showed

### Coverage was not the problem

70 of the 71 in-scope keywords already had an owning page before this session. The site holds 225 blog
posts, 143 university records and the programme hubs, and between them they cover every in-scope cluster
in the export. The single exception was `srm online mba` (1,300/mo, KD 6), which had no page at all.

The useful output of this export was therefore not a content backlog. It was a list of pages that already
rank and do not earn.

### Click-through, not ranking, is where the loss is

| Page | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Position |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `/programs/mba/healthcare-management` | 12,295 | 11 | 0.09% | 6.55 |
| `/programs/mba` | 6,926 | 11 | 0.16% | 6.93 |
| `/blog/best-online-mba-colleges-india-2026` | 3,897 | 15 | 0.38% | 6.18 |
| `/best-online-mba-india` | 875 | 5 | 0.57% | 11.75 |

Site-wide CTR is around 0.51%. Every page above sits on page one and converts below the site average,
and `/programs/mba/healthcare-management` is the fifth largest page on the site by impressions.

### Three pages are competing for one query cluster

`/programs/mba` carries the title "Best Online MBA Colleges in India 2026: Fees Compared". The blog post
`/blog/best-online-mba-colleges-india-2026` carries "Best Online MBA Colleges in India 2026: Top 15
UGC-DEB Ranked". `/best-online-mba-india` is a third page on the same intent. Together they take 11,698
impressions and 31 clicks, which is 0.26%, at an average position near 7.

The export shows why this matters: `best online mba colleges in india`, `best online mba institutes in
india`, `top online mba colleges in india` and `best online mba universities in india` are four separate
1,900/mo keywords at KD 0. They are one intent. Three pages split the signal for it.

This needs a consolidation decision (pick the canonical, redirect or de-target the other two) rather than
a silent edit, so it is recorded here and not actioned.

## Defects this export surfaced, and the fixes applied

**SRMIST claimed a NIRF rank it does not hold.** Its `tagline`, `description` and M.Com `careerOutcome`
all stated "NIRF #18 in University category 2025". Its own `nirf` field says 11, and Supabase
`accreditations` says University rank 11 of 200. #18 is SRMIST's Medical and Architecture rank. All three
strings now state #11 in the University category, and the Management rank of #56 alongside it. A scan of
all 143 records found this was the only prose-versus-data NIRF contradiction in the database.

**29 university descriptions were truncated at 45 characters.** Every one followed the template
"UGC DEB approved online programs from {institution}. NAAC {grade} accredited. ..." with the institution
name cut mid-word and the bracket left open, for example "from NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of
Management. NAAC A++ accredited." These render as body text on the university page, twice. All 29 are
rebuilt from the record's own `name`.

**4 university names were truncated mid-parenthesis.** `charusat-university-online`,
`vit-university-online`, `shobhit-university-online` and `sathyabama-university-online` rendered as
"Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology (Centre Online" and similar in every heading, title and
FAQ on their pages. Canonical names taken from Supabase `universities.name`. This also corrected VIT,
which was recorded as "Vellore Institute of Science and Technology" where Supabase has "Vellore Institute
of Technology".

## Open, deliberately not actioned here

1. **The three-page cannibalisation above.** A consolidation call, not a silent edit.
2. **Placeholder fees.** 100 of 143 university records share a fee range with at least one other record:
   59 sit on ₹60,000 to ₹2,00,000, 18 on 0 to 0, 11 on ₹75,000 to ₹1,80,000. Sathyabama's page title
   renders "Fees ₹60K-200K" straight out of that cluster. `getDisplayFee` resolves real per-programme
   fees for many of these, so the defect is the top-level field, not the whole record.
3. **15 em dashes remain in `lib/data.ts` taglines**, against house rule 1. They are inside the
   em-dash baseline, so they do not block a commit, and sweeping them is a separate pass.
4. **CTR rewrites** for the four underperforming pages in the table above. Worth doing, but each one is a
   title and meta change on a page with live Search Console history, so it should be measured as a cohort
   rather than changed ad hoc.
