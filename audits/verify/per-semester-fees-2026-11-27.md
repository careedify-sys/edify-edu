# Per-semester fee source table, B3 batch 26 (2026-11-27)

This table is for the fee gate only. It checks per-semester figures in blog posts. It never feeds page fees or titles.

**Input.** `m25.json` (engineer's measurement, batch 19 scratchpad). All 269 `PER_SEM_UNVERIFIED` entries were read. MISMATCH/UNRESOLVED entries were also included where the amount sits right next to "per semester", "/sem" or a per-semester column. They are grouped below by university and programme.

**Sources.** Only university portals and existing `audits/verify/` sheets were used. Rows taken from a sheet written today cite that sheet. Nothing was added or divided here. Where a blog figure equals a total divided by the number of semesters, that is noted as the likely origin, never as a source.

**Verdict key.**
- **SOURCED:** the blog figure equals the official per-semester figure.
- **CONFLICT:** the official figure differs. Both figures are given.
- **UNVERIFIABLE:** no official per-semester figure was found, or the page does not say what basis the figure is on.
