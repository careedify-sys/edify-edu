## Notes

- **Most serious:** the NAAC grade. Every structured surface (the record, data-slim, naac-verified.json, profile, hub, spec headers, meta descriptions and schema) says A++. The official list and Vignan's own portal say A+ (cycle 2, CGPA 3.49, declared 26-10-2021). The A++ values used on 4 Nov (cycle 4, 2024-06-16, CGPA 3.58) are SRM Institute of Science and Technology's NAAC row, so the Supabase join or row for Vignan is wrong. Any other university whose grade came from the same 4 Nov Supabase pass is worth a re-check against the xlsx. On top of that, the A+ cycle may have lapsed (Q37).
- **Second:** the fee. Every page prints ₹90,000, the EMI of ₹3,750 is ₹90,000/24, and seven spec pages call it the cheapest or most affordable option. Vignan publishes ₹1,10,000 as the MBA "Total Fee Plan". `data/fees-hub-data.json` already carries ₹1,10,000, so the site contradicts itself.
- The fee fix touches frozen or gated surfaces. The profile, hub and spec titles all contain "₹90,000". Check the protected-pages title freeze (to 5 Dec) and the blog fee gate before editing the Vignan review post. Per `feedback_blog_fee_gate_phrasing`, a reworded fee sentence fails the gate, so the post needs an allowlist decision rather than a silent edit.
- Sweep every fee notation (₹90,000 / ₹90K / Rs 90,000 / 90000 / "Rs 1 lakh"). Also sweep the derived peer gaps: peer JSONs say "Vignan at Rs 90,000 is Rs 18,500 cheaper" (arka-jain finance `:129`), "Rs 3,600" (Galgotias comparisons) and "Rs 4,000 cheaper" (logistics `:97`). All of them become wrong at ₹1,10,000 and must be removed, not recomputed.
- M.Sc Data Science is DEB-entitled for 2026-27 and listed on the portal, but absent from our record. The content freeze runs to 9 Jan, so record it only.
- Profile "Established N/A": the portal's disclosures list deemed-university status conferred in 2008. That can fill the field if Rishi wants it. It is not a regulator fact.
- `lib/data.ts:2366` description is cut mid-name ("Science, Technology &."). Same class as the 120-char tagline cut. Not a regulator fact.
- MBA `roles` (`:2375`) is finance-only (Financial Analyst, Tax Analyst, Investment Banking Trainee, Credit Analyst) and prints on the hub for a general MBA.
- Comparisons naming other universities (Uttaranchal, Sharda, IGNOU, SMU, Galgotias, DPU) were not checked here. They are out of scope.
