## Notes

- Most serious: UU's NAAC A+ was declared on 11-01-2022 and the NAAC list gives no end date. On a five-year cycle it lapses on 11-01-2027, eight weeks from today, yet `data/naac-verified.json` carries an unsourced 2029-01-11. Pages do not print the date, but they print the grade, and PAGE-STANDARDS allows NAAC only with a current cycle (Q28).
- The fee itself is right: every published UU figure agrees with our record. The faults sit around it: an EMI that is our own ÷24 (UU publishes ₹3,733, ours says ₹4,317), a marketed "30%" printed in a dozen places, "exam fee included (usually)" where UU says exam fee ₹2,500 per semester is included, and a missing annual line.
- The `programFees.mba` floor of ₹99,600 is UU's one-time figure, not the semester total; the record comment says this and it is correct.
- The salary range is labelled "alumni self-reported" on the hub. Nothing on UU's site or our files supports that label, which makes it worse than an unlabelled figure.
- The 8 UU spec JSONs carry 6 files with rupee-lakh figures and 7 with "2,500"; not rendered-checked today (the business-analytics spec page showed neither).
