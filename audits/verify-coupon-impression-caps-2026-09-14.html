What caps verify and coupons

Both clusters rank well and convert well. Neither gets shown. The reasons are different, and only one of them is worth your time.

edifyedu.in 7 days, finalised 1,538 clicks / 263,701 impressions site CTR 0.58%
The answer

One cluster has an SEO problem. The other has a market problem.

Coupons / demand cap

There is nothing to fix.

Total coupon-intent search demand across the whole site is roughly 33 impressions a week. You rank position 1 and 2 for the two real queries and convert at 60% and 25%. You have built 25 coupon pages to chase it.

Verify / self-inflicted cap

Three fixable causes.

Verify pages outrank your blog posts in 8 of 9 head-to-heads and convert 3.4x better. They still receive 5x fewer impressions, because your own blog posts are taking the SERP slot.

Cluster 2

Verify is capped three ways, and you control all three

123 verify pages sit in the sitemap. 63 have ever been shown. Of those 63, 44 earn zero clicks while burning 868 impressions a week.

The cluster averages position 6.07 and 1.84% CTR. The ranking is not the problem.

Every verify page emits the title {University} UGC-DEB Verification 2026 | EdifyEdu, from app/verify/[slug]/page.tsx:90. Students do not search that. They search whether a university is fake.

What consolidation is worth

3,261 blog impressions moved to the verify cluster's demonstrated 1.84% CTR, up from 0.71%, is +37 clicks a week, or roughly 160 a month. Redirect each is-X-fake-or-legit post into its verify page, and carry the "fake or legit" phrasing into the verify title so the surviving page keeps the query it was ranking for.

Cap three: the route is fragile under crawl

app/verify/[slug]/page.tsx:22 sets export const dynamic = 'force-dynamic' with no generateStaticParams. Every Googlebot hit on all 123 pages triggers a live Supabase round trip. A transient database failure returns notFound() at line 149, and generateMetadata falls through to the title "University Not Found" at line 75.

That is a plausible contributor to the 60 verify pages with zero impressions. Give the route generateStaticParams over verify-slugs.json and an ISR revalidate window, so a crawl reads a cached page instead of racing your database.
