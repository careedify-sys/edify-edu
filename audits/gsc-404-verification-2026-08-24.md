## Why the /verify URLs were failing

The verify pages themselves were never broken. They are driven by Supabase and they work.

The problem was the identifier. Supabase stores each university under its own `slug`, and `lib/data.ts` stores the same university under a different `id`. Those two strings **disagree for 63 of 128 universities**. Every internal verify link was built from the `id`, so for about half the catalogue it pointed at a slug Supabase has never heard of.

```
/verify/bharati-vidyapeeth-online              200   <- Supabase slug
/verify/bharati-vidyapeeth-university-online   404   <- lib/data.ts id
```

Of the 63 mismatches, **39** have a real verify page under a different slug and are now mapped and 301'd. The remaining **24** have no verify record in Supabase at all, so the link is now hidden rather than pointing at a 404.

The sitemap was never at fault: it lists 123 verify URLs straight from `verify-slugs.json`, and all 123 resolve.
