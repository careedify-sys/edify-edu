Stuck at position eight

Average position slipped 7.75 → 8.91 this month, but nothing is broken and there is no penalty. Impressions held flat. The blended number is misleading because more than half of it comes from calculator queries that Google answers on the results page itself. Strip those out and the real figure appears: the business pages sit at position 10.2, and only 0.7% of all impressions reach the top three. The site ranks. It does not get clicked.

What to do, in order

The plan

1

Stop tracking blended average position do today

It is not a health metric for this site. Half the input is calculator traffic that ranks well and never clicks, so the number moves for reasons that have nothing to do with the business.

In Search Console, filter out pages containing /tools/ and save that as your default view. The honest baseline is 445K impressions, 2,488 clicks, 0.56% CTR, position 10.2. Judge every future change against that, and watch clicks from fee queries rather than position.

2

Settle the blog-versus-hub fight on fee queries partly shipped

Pick one canonical page per "university + programme + fees" question, and it should be the hub, because that is where the current fee, the EMI split and the lead form live.

The 31 hubs given real fees this week already switched from a generic title to a fee-led one carrying the actual number, which is exactly what these searches want. What remains: point the matching blog review at its hub with a prominent link, and make sure the blog is not out-titling the hub for the same phrase. Start with Galgotias, BITS Pilani, Jain and Uttaranchal, worth about 9,000 impressions of misdirected demand between them.

3

Feed the page type that already converts

Specialisation pages return 1.22% against the hubs' 0.37% from the same position. They are the site's most efficient asset and they are under-linked.

Push internal links from hubs and blogs down into the specialisation pages, and give them the same care in title and description that the hubs just received. Bringing the 237 programme hubs up to specialisation-level CTR alone is worth roughly +520 clicks a month.

4

Work the striking-distance shortlist

67 non-tool pages hold 242,014 impressions at positions 5 to 12 and convert at 0.46%. They are already on page one; they are simply not being chosen.

At a modest 1.5% CTR that pool is worth +2,500 clicks a month with no new ranking required. Titles are already strong, so the lever is matching the page to the intent behind the query and earning the rich result, not rewriting copy. Biggest single items: the Galgotias review (16,854 impressions at 0.18%), the NAAC grades explainer (13,504 at 0.19%) and the MA full form guide (12,053 at 0.15%).

5

Stop investing in calculator content

It looks like half the site's traffic and is worth 1,396 clicks. Every additional CGPA variant adds impressions that will never convert and pushes the blended metrics further from reality.

Keep what exists, since it costs nothing to serve and earns some brand recall. Add nothing new.

Already in flight

What shipped this week that this report cannot see yet

This data window closes 23 August. The following went live on the 24th to 26th, so its effect lands in the next reporting period.

  • 35 hidden hub pages un-hidden, carrying about 36,000 monthly impressions that were previously set to noindex and therefore unrankable.
  • 31 of them now publish a real fee, which flips their page title to the fee-led format that targets the "university + fees" queries analysed above.
  • A site-wide fee display fix: the hero, specialisation cards and structured data were showing a placeholder range like "60K–200K" on pages with no verified fee. They now show "On request", and the schema no longer publishes a fabricated price.
  • Four pages remain on the counsellor form by design, where no published fee exists.

Expect impressions on those hubs to appear first and clicks to follow a few weeks later. Judge them on fee-query clicks, not on average position.
